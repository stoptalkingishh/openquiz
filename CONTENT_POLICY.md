# OpenQuiz content policy

OpenQuiz accepts original and properly licensed educational material. The catalog-review workflow is a review request, not automatic publication.

## What contributors must provide

For every catalog submission, provide a reviewable quiz link, a contact email, and one of the following:

- a statement that the work is original;
- a source link and the applicable license; or
- evidence that the copyright holder authorized public distribution.

The contributor must confirm that the material may be used, adapted, and distributed in the free public OpenQuiz catalog under terms compatible with AGPL-3.0-or-later.

## Material we do not accept

- Real, leaked, recalled, or confidential certification-exam questions or answer keys.
- Copyrighted textbooks, course packets, videos, slides, or question banks copied without permission.
- Personal information, credentials, private links, or confidential workplace material.
- Content that falsely claims endorsement by an exam vendor, school, employer, or author.

## Review and publication

Maintainers review scope, clarity, accessibility, factual quality, licensing, attribution, and rights. We may request changes, decline a submission, remove content, or correct attribution. An approved quiz is added through a pull request linked to its GitHub review Issue, preserving a public review trail.

Catalog placement does not imply that material is official, endorsed, complete, or suitable as a substitute for an exam vendor's authorized preparation resources.
